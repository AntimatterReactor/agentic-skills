Four commits between 2026-09-17 and 2026-10-12 had added about 3,800 lines, some of it unrelated work (CSV import, erase-all).

- **Docs are the heaviest-churn files**: TODO.md 32 commits, AGENTS.md 24, CLAUDE.md 14, SCHEME.md 12; `strings.xml` 25.
